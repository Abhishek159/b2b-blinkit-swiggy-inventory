What to do next

Use this page to decide whether a SKU is actually visible to customers in a locality.

  • Compare the same SKU on the other platform.
  • Switch area to find stockout pockets.
  • Use store rows for ops follow-up.
Blinkit stock is checked from your own browser, directly against blinkit.com. Instamart is checked from our server. Store locations come from a periodic snapshot, so a very new store may be missing.

How it works

Pick a platform, city, area and product. We then check every dark store in that area, one by one, and show what each one actually holds right now.

  • Available / Out of stock — the platform confirmed it.
  • Couldn't verify — the platform rate-limited or failed the check. That is not the same as out of stock, so we never count it as zero.
  • Not carried — that store's catalogue doesn't list the SKU at all.

Results are cached for an hour. If a live check gets rate-limited we show the last successful one and say how old it is.
