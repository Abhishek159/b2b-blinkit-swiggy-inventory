Store-level inventory

Exact evidence behind the availability map.

Blinkit stock is checked from your own browser, directly against blinkit.com. Instamart is checked from our server and cached for an hour. Store locations come from a periodic snapshot, so a very new store may be missing.

How Quick Commerce Atlas works

1. Choose the market

Select the platform, city and area you want to investigate.

2. Search a product

Use the customer-facing product name or a known SKU.

3. Read the evidence

Use the map for coverage and the store list for stock and price.

Every dark store in the chosen area is checked, one by one — not a sample. In stock and Out of stock mean the platform confirmed it. Not verified means the platform rate-limited or failed that check, which is not the same as out of stock — so those stores are never counted as zero. If a live check is rate-limited we show the last successful one and say how old it is.
